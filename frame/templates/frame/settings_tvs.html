TVs

The Frame TVs Frame has found. Turn a TV on and Frame finds it within 10 minutes, or press Look now.

‹ Frame TV settings

To have Frame leave a TV alone, tick it under "Leave these TVs alone" in Frame TV settings.

Pair

Frame needs the TV's permission once. Do this at the TV, with its remote:

  1. Turn the TV on so the picture is showing (TV or Art Mode). A TV in standby can't ask.
  2. Let the TV ask about new devices. On the TV go to Settings → All Settings → Connection → External Device Manager → Device Connection Manager and set Access Notification to First Time Only. If "BDH Frame" is under Device List there as Deny, change it to Allow (or delete it) first.
  3. Press Ask the TV below, then press Allow in the box that pops up on the TV within seconds.
Tip: to keep the TV reachable when it's off, turn on Settings → All Settings → General → Network → Expert Settings → Power On with Mobile.
